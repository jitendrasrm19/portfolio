Analytics Decision Pack

This sample shows how I connect a business question to SQL, KPI definitions, dashboard design, and stakeholder insight. It is not a client production dashboard.

Business question

Where are operational records accumulating, and which workflow stages should stakeholders investigate first?

SQL analysis pattern

SELECT workflow_stage,
       COUNT(*) AS record_count,
       AVG(processing_hours) AS avg_processing_hours
FROM workflow_events
GROUP BY workflow_stage
ORDER BY avg_processing_hours DESC;

KPI definitions

KPIDefinition
VolumeNumber of records entering each workflow stage.
Average processing timeMean elapsed processing hours by stage.
Exception rateExceptions divided by total processed records.

Dashboard wireframe

Top row: Total volume | Avg. processing time | Exception rate
Middle: Volume by workflow stage | Processing-time trend
Bottom: Exception category breakdown | Detail table for investigation

Stakeholder takeaway

Use the dashboard to identify stages with both high volume and high processing time, then drill into exception categories before proposing a process change.
